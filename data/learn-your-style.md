## Hero

**H1:** Learn Your Style

**Lede:** Colours in Magic are play personalities. A white pile and a red pile ask you to win in different ways. This lesson is about how each colour (and a few common pairs) usually feels on the table — not a rules dump.

**CTA:** Prefer to learn by playing? → Play

## The five colours

### White
White likes order, small efficient creatures, and protecting life. You often go wide with several creatures, gain life, or lock the board down with removal that feels “fair.” Good first feel: clear board, attack together.

### Blue
Blue likes answers and information. You draw extra cards, bounce or counter threats, and win by having more options than your opponent. Good first feel: say “not yet,” then pull ahead with card advantage.

### Black
Black spends resources — including life — to get ahead. You remove creatures, make the opponent discard, and close with something nasty. Good first feel: trade life or cards for control of the game.

### Red
Red is fast and direct. You deal damage to players and creatures, attack early, and finish before the opponent stabilises. Good first feel: point at the face, keep the pressure on.

### Green
Green grows. You play bigger creatures, find lands, and overwhelm with size. Good first feel: get more mana than them, then cast something that ends the argument.

## Common pairs (what they feel like together)

You do not need these memorised. When a sleeved 60 or Pool Party mix shows two colours, this is the vibe:

- **White–Blue** — flyers, tempo, and polite control. Chip away from the air while answering threats.
- **Blue–Black** — discard, counters, and attrition. Make them play with fewer cards.
- **Black–Red** — aggression with removal. Damage now, clean up blockers.
- **Red–Green** — big attacks and mana. Hit hard; sometimes hit even harder next turn.
- **Green–White** — tokens and mid-sized creatures. Build a team, then swing.
- **White–Black** — life drain, efficient creatures, and removal. Grind value.
- **Blue–Red** — spells and tricks. Instants, card draw, sudden damage.
- **Black–Green** — graveyard and big threats. Recycle; grow into monsters.
- **Red–White** — go-wide attacks and burn. Lots of creatures, finish with direct damage.
- **Green–Blue** — extra mana and extra cards. Get ahead on resources, then cast the problem.

Mono-colour decks (one colour only) are simpler to learn. Two-colour decks add more tools and more mana questions.

## How this maps to our till

- **Pool Party** modules are single-colour twenty-card packs. Mix two colours and you feel a pair in one game.
- **Beginner Casual / Standard** sleeved 60s list their colours on the product card — pick the personality you want to try.
- You do not need the perfect colour theory before you shuffle. Pick a vibe, play a game, switch next time.
