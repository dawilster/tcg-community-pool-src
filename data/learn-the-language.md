## Creature keywords

**Flying** — This creature can only be blocked by creatures with flying or reach. It attacks “over” ground creatures.

**Reach** — Can block creatures with flying.

**Haste** — Can attack (and use tap abilities) the turn it enters. Skips summoning sickness for attacking.

**Vigilance** — Attacks without tapping. Still ready to block afterward.

**Trample** — Extra combat damage beyond what blockers can absorb goes to the defending player.

**Deathtouch** — Any amount of combat damage from this creature is enough to destroy the creature it damages.

**Lifelink** — Damage this creature deals also gains you that much life.

**Menace** — Can only be blocked by two or more creatures.

**Flash** — You may cast this when you could cast an instant — including on the opponent’s turn.

**Ward** — If an opponent targets this with a spell or ability, they must pay an extra cost (often mana) or the spell/ability is countered. Exact cost is on the card.

## Board and spells

**Tap / untap** — Turn sideways to use; straighten on your untap step. Tapped creatures usually can’t block.

**Cast** — Pay the cost and put the spell onto the stack (for learning: “play it”).

**Permanent** — A card that stays on the battlefield (lands, creatures, artifacts, enchantments, planeswalkers).

**Instant** — A spell you can often cast on either turn, when you have priority.

**Sorcery** — A spell you cast on your turn, during a main phase, when the stack is empty (for learning: on your turn, not in combat tricks territory until you’re ready).

**Enchantment / Artifact** — Permanents that aren’t creatures (unless the card says otherwise). They sit there and change the game.

**Token** — A creature (or other permanent) made by a spell or ability, not from your deck. Leaves when it dies; doesn’t go to your graveyard as a card.

## How players talk about plans

**Removal** — A spell or ability that destroys, exiles, or otherwise gets rid of a permanent (often a creature).

**Ramp** — Effects that put extra lands into play or make extra mana so you cast bigger spells sooner.

**Counters** — Markers on a permanent. +1/+1 counters make a creature stronger; other counters do what the card says. Not the same as “countering a spell.”

**Counter (a spell)** — An effect that stops a spell on the stack so it doesn’t happen. Usually blue.

**Sacrifice** — You choose a permanent you control and put it into the graveyard as a cost or effect. You can’t sacrifice something you don’t control.

**Draw** — Put the top card of your library into your hand.

**Discard** — Put a card from your hand into your graveyard.

**Mill** — Put cards from a library into a graveyard (usually the opponent’s). Less common in first games.

## Numbers on a creature

**Power / Toughness** — Written like 2/3. Power is damage dealt in combat; toughness is how much damage it can take before it dies that turn.

**Mana value** — Roughly “how expensive is this?” — the total mana in the cost, counting coloured symbols as one each. Useful when comparing cards; you don’t need it to play your first game.
