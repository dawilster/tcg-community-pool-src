## Hero

**H1:** About Community Pool

**Lede:** Magic is hard to start and hard to leave cleanly. New players hit a steep first step. Established players end up with boxes of good cards and no simple way to move them without becoming a shop. Community Pool is one place for both problems.

---

## Two problems, one pool

**Getting in.** There is very little between “I’m interested” and buying a full deck, learning complex rules, and sitting down at a store where you may get badly beaten. There is no obvious swim school — nowhere to test the waters at a comfortable pace and budget.

**Getting bulk moving.** Most leftover commons and uncommons are still playable, but listing, pricing, packing, and postage for every card is more work than the cards are worth. So they sit as dead stock. Buyers chasing a few cheap singles often pay postage several times across sellers — or hit a $1 floor that warps the low end.

**What we do.** Community Pool centralises inventory into one Cairns warehouse and one storefront. New players get Pool Party, Floaties, and sleeved beginner decks. Collectors see Set Availability. Everyone gets one catalogue and one postage. Later, contributors can put eligible cards into the same Pool and get paid when those cards sell — without building their own store.

---

## Why this exists

The founder came back to Magic through Universes Beyond and hit the usual friction: which format, which cards, how to learn, and why a handful of cheap singles somehow cost more in shipping than in cardboard. Community Pool is the response — a soft entry for new players and shared infrastructure so ordinary players don’t have to become retailers.

## Phase 1, honestly

Right now we sell **Jamie’s owned inventory** only. The storefront, swimming count, singles case, and curated products are real proofs. Public Contribute and My Pool are **Coming Soon** — we are testing multi-owner FIFO with simulated crates first. We will not promise a date, payout %, or legal treatment on this page.

Commander piles are not on the till in Phase 1; sealed precons already cover that shelf. If demand shows up, we may expand.
